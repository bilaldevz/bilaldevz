## A little about me

I'm a Data Science student at the **University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore**, currently exploring a bit of everything that interests me.

I enjoy the process of taking a random thought, spotting a real problem behind it, and then trying to build something around it.

I'm particularly interested in:

* Data Science and AI
* Software and web development
* Robotics and intelligent systems
* Algorithms and problem solving
* Turning small ideas into usable products

I'm still learning, still experimenting, and occasionally breaking things that were working perfectly fine five minutes ago.

That's basically how most of my projects start.
